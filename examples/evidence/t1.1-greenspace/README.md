# T1.1 — Greenspace exposure inequality (PROPOSED)

Chen et al., *Contrasting inequality in human exposure to greenspace between cities of Global North and Global South*, Nature Communications 13:4636 (2022). The article is CC BY 4.0. The city-level deposit is **CC BY-NC 4.0** (HKU DataHub / Figshare article 19914988, confirmed 2026-09-12 via the public Figshare API).

The paper reports mean city-level Ginis of **0.47** (Global South, n=506) and **0.24 ± 0.13** (Global North, n=522), from the exposure formula `GEd = Σ(Pi × Gid) / ΣPi`. Reproducing those means needs the per-city Gini attributes in `Global_1028_cities_GE_Gini.shp`.

That extract is **not** in this repository. moveq is BSD-3-Clause; CC BY-NC forbids commercial reuse, so committing the table would make the public repo unsafe to use commercially. The DataHub HTML project page also returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch; that is a retrieval inconvenience, not the reason the files are absent.

This case stays **PROPOSED** until a committable extract exists under a redistribution-compatible licence. It is not REPRODUCED. `concentrationMetrics` may be cited as the authors' tool; gallery checks use `oracles.py`.
